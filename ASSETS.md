# Visual assets

The wordmark, sprout symbol, arrows, and seal are project-native SVG artwork in `src/App.tsx` and `public/favicon.svg`.

Fonts are distributed by the Fontsource packages and bundled with the application. Their license files are included with the installed packages: DM Sans and Instrument Serif use the SIL Open Font License.

## Hero photograph

`public/images/hero-greenhouse.webp` is generated illustrative photography using the built-in Image Generation tool. It does not depict an actual Horticulture Industries facility.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website hero photograph, portrait orientation. Create an editorial photograph of the inside of a lush botanical greenhouse. A central narrow terracotta and pale stone aisle leads between layered ferns, small palms, and sculptural broadleaf plants in beautiful weathered terracotta pots on low growing benches. Elegant aged white-painted glasshouse architecture, tall windows and a pitched glass roof, warm morning sunlight coming from the upper right, soft atmospheric shadows and authentic leaf texture. Palette: deep forest green, fresh green, warm cream, subtle terracotta. Composition: immersive natural photograph with strong depth, some close leaves in the foreground, a visually rich upper canopy, no large empty space. Calm, tactile, sophisticated horticulture editorial, natural film photography, true-to-life materials. No people, no signs, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no borders. This is a standalone photograph, not a website mockup.
